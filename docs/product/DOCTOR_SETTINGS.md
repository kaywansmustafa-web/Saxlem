# Doctor Settings

Sprint 12I provides a read-only doctor identity and settings foundation. It helps the doctor understand the active identity, clinic assignment, current session, future application and notification preferences, environment, and support destinations without exposing administration or editing.

All preference controls are disabled and explicitly labeled “Available in a future release.” Support destinations are honest informational placeholders. The only active controls are Return to Workspace, Open Schedule, and Open Notifications.

Profile editing, authentication, passwords, persistence, billing, reports, analytics, schedule changes, queue changes, patient changes, appointment changes, and Flutter work are excluded.
