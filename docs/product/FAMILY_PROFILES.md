# Current Patient

Sprint 11 MVP lets one authenticated account add family members and choose the
patient whose care is currently being managed. Product copy uses “Current
Patient,” “Choose Patient,” and “Booking For”; the internal domain name is
`PatientProfile`.

The active patient controls Dashboard identity, Booking, Appointments,
Notifications, and Live Queue. Notifications are automatically filtered, while
account-wide updates remain visible. Switching uses a subtle identity crossfade,
not a full-screen animation.

This MVP intentionally excludes editing, deletion, notes, phone numbers, accent
selection, persistence, and backend family authorization.
