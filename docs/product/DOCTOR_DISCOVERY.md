# Doctor Discovery Product Specification

Doctor Discovery helps Saxlem patients find appropriate fictional demonstration doctors and clinics using doctor names, specialties, clinic names, and everyday health terms. It is search assistance, not diagnosis.

The experience supports expandable city/area locations, specialty, gender, integer-IQD fee, availability, wait, language, and verification filters. Sorting supports recommendation, earliest availability, shortest wait, nearest, lowest fee, and highest rating.

Patient-facing availability uses: **Available Now**, **Available Today**, **Tomorrow**, and **Fully Booked**. Ratings distinguish total ratings from written reviews. Favorite membership is presented as the foundation of **My Doctors**.

All current provider and clinic records are fictional demonstration data. Production data must be verified before public release.

Required states are initial, loading, results, empty, filtered-empty, stale, offline, and failure. Layouts must remain usable in English, Badini Kurdish, Arabic, and future Badini Latin, under RTL and 200% text scaling.
