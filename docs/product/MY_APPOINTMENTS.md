# My Appointments

Sprint 6 gives patients a single, calm place to view upcoming, completed, and cancelled visits. Each tab displays its appointment count. Upcoming cards surface the clinic, date, time, status, appointment ID, consultation duration, and an estimated wait when one exists.

Appointment details show the clinical and financial summary. Future visits explain that the queue opens on appointment day; eligible same-day visits offer **Open Live Queue**.

Empty states distinguish a patient who has never booked from a returning patient with no appointments in the selected category. Both lead to doctor discovery.

This sprint intentionally excludes cancellation, rescheduling, follow-up actions, payments, notifications, and changes to Live Queue behavior. Rate Visit and Book Again remain non-destructive placeholders.
