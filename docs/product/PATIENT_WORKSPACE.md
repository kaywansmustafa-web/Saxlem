# Patient Workspace

The receptionist Patients section is a task workspace, not a patient database or CRUD surface. Its fixed hierarchy is Search, Today's Arrivals, Recently Viewed, and All Patients. Search filters mock data immediately by first name, last name, full name, normalized phone digits, or patient ID. Arrow keys move through results, Enter opens the selected workspace, and Escape clears search.

Each patient workspace has no tabs or nested navigation. It shows identity, today's appointment, upcoming appointments, family members, recent notifications, and exactly three read-only destination actions. Sprint 12B contains no check-in, editing, deletion, persistence, backend, or operational mutations.
