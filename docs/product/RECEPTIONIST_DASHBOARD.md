# Receptionist Dashboard

The fixed hierarchy is Clinic Status, four metrics, Live Queues, Next Arrivals, Doctors Working, Important Updates, and exactly three Quick Actions. Metrics are today's appointments, waiting patients, checked-in patients, and delayed doctors. Delayed queues precede the longest remaining queue. Tables expose identity, time, doctor, status, and next step without hidden menus or color-only meaning.

Acceptance requires a first-time receptionist to identify the next arrival, busiest doctor, longest queue, and delay state within 30 seconds. Loading, empty, failure, and unavailable-session states explain what is happening and what to do next.
