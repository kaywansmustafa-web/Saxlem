# Appointment Workspace

Sprint 12C provides a read-only operational schedule. Its order is Today's Schedule, Needs Attention, Upcoming, and All Today's Appointments. Search filters immediately by patient, doctor, phone, or appointment ID. The workspace has no tabs and exposes summary, patient, doctor, clinic, timeline, queue information, a notes placeholder, and exactly three navigation actions. No editing, check-in, cancellation, rescheduling, persistence, or queue mutation exists.
