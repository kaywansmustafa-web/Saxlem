# Doctor Workspace

Sprint 12F provides a calm, role-specific workspace for one doctor during an active clinic session. Within ten seconds the doctor should understand the current patient, elapsed consultation time, next patients, queue condition, and safest next action.

The page order is fixed: session status, current patient, appointment context, next patients, queue health, recent activity, and doctor actions. Current Patient is visually dominant. Only pause, resume, complete consultation, and finish session are supported. Completion and finish require confirmation.

The appointment context is deliberately narrow. It includes scheduling, arrival, queue history, and a non-clinical reception note. Diagnoses, prescriptions, medical history, clinical notes, billing, editing, cancellation, and rescheduling are excluded.

English and Arabic catalogs are structurally complete. New Badini doctor terminology is retained in English for native review rather than machine-translated.
